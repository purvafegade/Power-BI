# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Analysis


# Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze employee data and present important HR insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard. It helps HR departments and management understand workforce performance, employee satisfaction and attrition patterns.


## Dataset Description

The HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Analysis project uses an HR dataset containing employee-related information. The dataset consists of five tables: Employee, Education Level, Performance Rating, Rating Level, and Satisfied Level.


## Dataset Columns

| Table Name | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Employee | Stores employee-related information, including personal and professional details. |
| Education Level | Stores employee education categories and qualification details. |
| Performance Rating | Stores employee performance information and evaluation details. |
| Rating Level | Stores different performance rating categories and levels. |
| Satisfied Level | Stores employee satisfaction categories and satisfaction levels. |


## Project Overview

- Overall employee count and workforce distribution.
- Employee attrition rate and total employees who left the organization.
- Department-wise employee attrition and turnover.
- Gender-wise employee distribution and attrition patterns.
- Age-wise employee attrition and workforce trends.
- Job role-wise attrition and employee turnover.
- Education level and its relationship with employee attrition.
- Employee job satisfaction and satisfaction levels.
- Performance ratings and their impact on employee retention.
- Average age and salary of employees who left the organization.
- Key factors influencing employee attrition and employee retention.

## Page 1 – HR Analytics Overview

#Cards (KPI)

1. Total Employees
2. Active Employees
3. Employees Left
4. Attrition Rate
5. Average Monthly Salary
6. Total Departments
   
# Visuals

1. Employee Attrition Status – Donut Chart
- Shows employees who stayed and employees who left.
2. Employee Distribution by Department – Pie Chart
- Shows the number of employees in each department.
3. Employee Distribution by Gender – Donut Chart
- Shows male and female employee distribution.
4. Employees by Job Role – Clustered Column Chart
- Compares the number of employees across different job roles.
5. Average Monthly Salary vs Employee Count by Department – Scatter Chart
- Compares salary levels and employee counts across departments.
# Slicers

1. Department
2. Gender
3. Job Role
4. Education Level

**Purpose:** Understand the overall workforce by analyzing total employees, active employees, attrition rate, department distribution, gender distribution, and job roles.
